# Lyapunov 0.1.0-alpha.6.1

[English README](../../README.md) · [中文 README](../../README.zh-CN.md) · [Installation / 安装](https://vorynel.com/lyapunov/)

## English

This Linux x64 Alpha keeps the owned DSH fork pinned to `0.2.0-rc.2` and improves native robot assembly and position-control declarations.

- Fixed-base attachments use native parent-body assembly. A dexterous hand attached to an arm follows its selected link without relying on a soft weld. Missing targets, self-targets and cyclic attachments are rejected.
- Pure-torque motor models require explicit position-loop gains before position commands. Importing G1 does not manufacture a standing or walking controller; load a compatible, licensed policy or declare the intended controller before motion.
- Unanchored fixed-base models can be lifted out of an initial ground overlap with a recorded warning. Explicit world or body anchors retain their requested positions.
- The application version shown on the welcome page now comes from the same frozen version as the release manifest. The installer keeps the previous version and user data.

The package includes the managed MuJoCo runtime. Isaac remains optional and requires its own supported runtime, driver and GPU. Large environment collision derivation may take time and memory; a visible mesh alone does not prove that its collision or physics is ready.

## 中文

此 Linux x64 Alpha 继续锁定自有 DSH fork 的 `0.2.0-rc.2`，改进原生机器人装配与位置控制声明。

- 固定基座使用原生父连杆装配。安装在机械臂上的灵巧手跟随指定连杆，不依赖软焊接。缺失目标、自身目标和循环挂载会明确拒绝。
- 纯力矩电机模型执行位置指令前必须显式声明位置环增益。导入 G1 不会凭空生成站立或行走控制器；运动前需加载适配且获许可的策略，或声明所需控制器。
- 未显式锚定的固定基座模型可从初始地面重叠中抬升，并记录警告。显式世界或连杆锚点保留请求的位置。
- 欢迎页显示的应用版本与发行清单采用同一份冻结版本。安装器保留上一版本和用户数据。

安装包包含独立管理的 MuJoCo 运行环境。Isaac 为可选功能，需要受支持的独立运行环境、驱动和 GPU。大型环境的碰撞派生可能需要较多时间和内存；能够看到网格，并不代表碰撞和物理已就绪。
